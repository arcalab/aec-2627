What is happening

Complexity: heapify is O(N) (each node is pushed down at most proportional to its height, and most nodes are near the bottom); ordenaHeap performs N−1 extractions, each O(log N) — total O(N log N), in place, not stable. With a min-heap, each extracted minimum is parked at the end of the array, so the result is in descending order; to sort ascending, mirror the comparisons (max-heap) or reverse the array in a final O(N) pass.
