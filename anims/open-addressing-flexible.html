Why does deletion leave a tombstone?

A search may need to pass through a position where a key used to be. If deletion changed that position back to never used, a later search could stop there and incorrectly report that a key further along the probe sequence is absent.

A tombstone is therefore different from an empty slot: searches continue through it, while insertions may reuse it. When inserting into a tombstone, the algorithm continues probing until it can rule out an existing copy of the key.

Probe formula: pᵢ = (h(k) + i) mod m, where h(k) = k mod m and m is the table size.
